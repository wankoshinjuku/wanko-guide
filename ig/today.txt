What is wanko soba? 🍜 A food tradition from Iwate: bite-sized soba, one bowl after another.
Kurukuru Wanko is Japan's first rotating wanko soba in Shinjuku.
📍1 min from Seibu-Shinjuku Station / 4 min from JR Shinjuku (East Exit)
🕚 Daily 11:00–18:30 (last entry) · Walk-ins in order of arrival
👶 Preschoolers free (one per accompanying adult) · Halal-friendly
